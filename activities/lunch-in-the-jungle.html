Lunch in the Jungle

Step into the heart of Balinese village life through this unique jungle cooking experience — a day filled with nature, tradition, and togetherness. Your adventure begins with a guided walk through the jungle to the riverside.

The night before, our local team sets traditional fish traps in the river. In the morning, you'll help check the traps and collect your fresh catch of the day, picking vegetables, herbs and spices straight from the forest along the way.

You're invited to join the cooking process and learn how to make authentic local dishes over a wood-fired stove, just like the locals do — fish or shrimp from the river and vegetables from the jungle. Before lunch, indulge in a refreshing bath in a natural spring.
